### 2. Curation & Contact-Tracking Workflow
The tracking sheet is colour-coded:

- **Yellow:** missing contact info
- **Blue:** contacted, waiting for reply

Logging workflow:

1. Copy the record ID.
2. Paste it into the log.
3. Enter the contact date and channel (email, DM, WhatsApp or phone).
4. Add notes and replies.

The status and colour change after logging. After contacting someone from the main sheet, always map and log it so everything stays centralized.

### 5. Public Registration & Call for Makers
Registrations are currently starting within the TinkerHub community, but a public-facing mechanism is needed:

- An **Interest to Attend** form where anyone can express intent, so the team can later map and send invitations.
- A **Call for Makers** (and possibly a **Call for Exhibitions**), launched as the standard Maker Faire process.
- A simple platform or form is enough for now.
- Once the form is live, contacts are directed to fill it in instead of the team collecting details manually during outreach.

> [!IMPORTANT] Decision
> Launch a public Interest to Attend form and a Call for Makers on the Maker Faire website. Once live, use the form as the primary data-collection link in all outreach.
