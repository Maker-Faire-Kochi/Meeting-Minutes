## Discussions & Key Decisions

### 1. Content Plan & Formats
Three content formats can be produced quickly:

- **Short-form videos** (15–30 seconds)
- **Carousel posts**, either project-focused or from a "maker profile" angle
- **Event updates and quick posts** (motion posters, basic announcements)

"Human touch" content (people speaking, creators featured) is likely to get better reach than posters alone. Faces and hosts will be rotated to avoid overexposure (for example alternating Jasim, Abraham and others), and other accounts such as "Tinker Dot Malayalam" can be used too.

### 2. Content Scheduling & Pipeline
A proper schedule/content calendar is needed, driven by:

- A list of partner communities and teams (e.g. MakerGram, TinkerHub and related partners)
- A pipeline for welcoming and onboarding new communities and featuring what they do

A **"Road to Maker Faire Kochi"** idea was also discussed: roadshows or flash activations in major cities such as Trivandrum, Kochi and Calicut.

### 3. Tinkercad Access Constraint
The tool is account-based, so sharing access is difficult without access to the account email. **Workaround:** use the tool directly on the machine/browser session, with limited access for a single person (Akshay).

### 4. Collaborations & Creator Outreach
- **"Explain the Planet"-style collaboration:** a video on Kerala's maker culture with a call to action for Maker Faire.
- **"Malayali.info"-style collaboration** (similar category).
- **Calicut short-form content:** a proposal to produce about 10 reels in a week around Calicut makers. Limits need to be realistic since this is early stage.
- **Coders Cafe (Kannur):** another creator with strong reach (a chicken coop video performed well). More such creators will be identified and routed through Samad for outreach.

### 5. Curation & Outreach Workflow (Google Sheets)
Two sheets are in use:

1. **"Raw Maker Faire" sheet:** a compiled dump with scoring and fields, prepared by Abhiram using an agent/workflow and then structured.
2. **"Curator List" sheet:** the working shortlist, with individuals and organizations separated.

**Goal:** select about 200 makers/organizations from the raw list into the Curator List.

**Workflow:**

1. Review the candidate's profile (Instagram, LinkedIn or portfolio).
2. If aligned, add to the Curator List using the unique **Maker ID**.
3. DM or message as a Maker Faire representative, inviting them to participate or present.
4. Update the status in the sheet and assign an **Owner** (who reached out).

Not all entries have an email. Outreach may start on social media, with the email collected afterwards.

**Sheet improvements:**

- Add a **"Last Contacted On" / "Last Updated"** column, automated with a timestamp function tied to status edits.
- The **Owner** field already exists, which prevents overlap and duplicate claiming.
- Avoid duplicating full data across sheets: copy only name and Maker ID into the Curator List.

### 8. Partner Onboarding Tracking
There is no formal partner onboarding flow today. A dedicated **"Partners" sheet** will list all existing partners, mapped by domain/type. When a partner is onboarded, the media team will be pinged so content and features can be planned.

### 9. Decisions & Agreements

- Use a structured Google Sheets curation workflow to shortlist about 200 makers/organizations and track outreach ownership and status.
- Use the **Owner** approach to prevent duplicated outreach, and add a "Last Contacted/Updated" timestamp.
- Create a Partners sheet, and make partner onboarding trigger a media-team ping.
