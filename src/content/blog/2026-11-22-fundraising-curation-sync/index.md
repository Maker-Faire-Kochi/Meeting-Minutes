## Discussions & Key Decisions

### 1. Fundraising & Sponsorship Status
**Solid leads so far:** Starter Machine and TinkerHub (TinkerHub was mentioned as confirmed).

- **Starter Machine:** Handling venue coordination directly with Chakkolas, with the advance/payment process running in parallel. The event is positioned officially under Starter Machine's umbrella and program naming (public-facing submission and program references were mentioned).
- **Singapore-based fundraising group** (India presence, Calicut subsidiary): will approach companies with marketing budgets. Commission is **10% of funds raised**.
- **Dubai-based design/architecture network** (linked to design firms and institutes around Calicut and Wayanad): will not raise funds directly, but can introduce good potential sponsors.
- **Sponsorship tiers:** The earlier tiers (13 / 15 / 20 lakhs etc.) were judged ineffective. A new proposal with different slabs has been created, **starting at ~50 lakhs**, and is being shared with the team (Susan and others).

> [!IMPORTANT] Fundraising blockers
> The fundraising team needs two things before they can pitch properly: (1) a clear **2-day event schedule/agenda** and (2) a **sponsor branding inventory** showing where logos appear (stage backdrop, standees, ID cards, venue placements, etc.).

### 2. Venue, Layout & Design
> [!IMPORTANT] Venue finalised
> The venue is finalised as **Chakkolas**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Decision
> Use **only the two godown/warehouse spaces** for the event setup. The outer area is kept mainly for parking. This reduces cost and avoids expensive structures and finishes such as premium hangars.

- **Cost reference:** Another architecture/design community event at the same venue reportedly spent 60–70 lakhs in total. Air-conditioning alone was ~15 lakhs and cooling was still insufficient.
- **Measurements:** That event's drawings and measurements will not be shared. If an existing set cannot be obtained from the venue or others, a survey team will be arranged for fresh baseline measurements.
- **Design:** Asif will produce a base venue design and zoning draft. An early estimate was 2–3 days; later discussion pointed to design refinement by next weekend.
- **Interactive layout visualization (Abhiram):** The existing concept lets users click an area (workshops, community space, etc.) to see details. It will be reworked to the new measurements and final zoning, and is useful for sponsor communication (branding placement and program zones).

### 3. Program, Curation & Content Planning
**Program clarity for fundraising:** Enough of the program needs to be locked to support pitches: keynotes, inauguration and closing, number and type of sessions, and a possible cultural/art performance (relevant for pitches to government bodies such as KTDC).

**Curation approach:** Start curation now without waiting for exact stall or workshop counts.

- **Expected scale:** about 40–50 workshops and 10+ stalls, with flexibility for day-1-only or day-2-only participants.
- **Spaces:** Student space, Hobbyists (the "HBC/OBC" reference was unclear and is treated as the hobbyist/community segment), and Startup/Tech space.
- **Support for participants:** The current budget covers food on event days. Accommodation and travel are not covered, but may be considered if fundraising improves.

**Maker database and tools**

- Master list of ~1,500 makers, with ~298 emails currently available. A separate mention said 198 emails were used for the blast, so this needs reconciling.
- An email blast inviting makers to showcase projects has gone out, with ~6 bounces.
- The internal web tool has spaces, rough schedules and **26 main-stage sessions across both days** (a projection, not confirmed). It is not public yet (email restriction in place) and is planned to open 1–2 weeks before the event. Teams and entries are tagged, and clicking one shows the full profile.

**Curation workflow:** Create a "Curated" sheet/tab derived from the master database, with these fields:

- Reference/Record ID
- Category fit (workshop / exhibit / talk)
- City/state
- Website, email and socials
- Reviewer/owner ("On-by")
- Outreach status
- Notes
- Last update

Ownership is assigned through "On-by" so multiple people do not duplicate outreach.

### 4. Marketing, Social & Media Outreach
- **Content strategy:** Move beyond repetitive posters to short explainer videos and reels. Publish clear public messaging covering hands-on exhibits, workshops, talks and panels, and who the event is for (students, hobbyists, engineers, etc.).
- **Media outreach:** A media outreach sheet exists in the Drive. Featuring selected makers and projects in media before the event can build momentum.
- **Amplification:** Ask known creators and accounts to post, reshare or collab on posts announcing the event. Raw, simple content is acceptable and brand guidelines are optional.

### 5. Decisions & Agreements
- **Daily sync:** The team meets daily at **8:30 PM** (PM implied in the recording) to keep curation and outreach moving.
- **Start curation now:** Initial working target of ~**200 individual makers + ~100 organizations**.
- **Outreach flow:** Start with a short DM or message, then follow up with an official email if needed, especially for organizations.
