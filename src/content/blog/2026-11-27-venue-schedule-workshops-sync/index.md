## Discussions & Key Decisions

### 1. Venue
- The venue is confirmed, and Start Mission has sent the payment. It covers about **33,000 sq. ft.**
- A venue team will share the floor plan and elevations within two to three days. The team will use them to plan stall layouts, installation locations and space allocation.
- The design quote is about **₹1.50 per sq. ft.** for a floor plan alone, or **₹2.50 per sq. ft.** with elevations.

> [!IMPORTANT] Decision
> The venue is confirmed and paid for. Space planning will start once the floor plan and elevations arrive.

### 2. Two-Day Event Schedule
A high-level two-day schedule is needed. It should outline the opening, closing, stalls, workshops and talk sessions. Detailed programming is not needed yet.

### 3. Stall Design & Event Management
- The team is considering custom stall designs. Final measurements and design details depend on the venue plan.
- An event-management team can be brought in once the design is ready.

### 4. Sponsorship
Key Value has offered sponsorship of up to **₹1 lakh** for activities related to student engineering and hobbies. The team needs to develop sponsorship tiers and ideas for this.

### 5. Workshops & Children's Activities
- The team discussed workshops and activities for children under 18, including science demonstrations and hands-on experiences.
- Possible partner organisations should be explored, including the Little Big Festival team.

### 6. Company Partners & Installations
- Engineering companies, startups and other organisations could be invited to present products, run workshops or offer hands-on activities. They could also be listed as company partners.
- The team is considering creative and engineering installations, along with ways to explain how they work, such as interactive or "exploded view" displays.
- Possible connections: the shipyard team, SFO, V-Guard and the Little Big Festival team.

### 7. Social Media & Fab Lab Videos
- Social media content is in progress.
- The team plans to record short videos at the Fab lab that show people's projects and explain individual machines.
- Videos should be **under 60 seconds** and introduce each machine and what it is used for.

### 8. T-Shirts & Merchandise
T-shirt and related design work is underway. Jasim will share sample options in the group for feedback.
